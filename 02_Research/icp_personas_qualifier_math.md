Founder rate 175 USD per hour. Midpoint between 150 and 200.
Hours to cover cost: 39,000 divided by 175 equals 223 hours per year. 18.6 hours per month. 4.3 hours per week.
Pareto saves 12.5 hours per week. Source paretotalent.com ROI block.
Net gain: 12.5 minus 4.3 equals 8.2 hours per week. Value 1,435 USD per week. Payback in 27 weeks at full load, faster if rate is 200.
