Title: Target Shift. Older Companies Over Digital Entrepreneurs. Owner Demian Ducardt. Date 2026-10-07.

Thesis: the Delegation Score funnel chases established local companies such as trades, clinics, firms, local services. It does not chase digital entrepreneurs such as coaches plus creators. Four cited reasons follow.

Reason 1. Behind on average, so the test hits harder.
Small firms trail large firms in AI use by about a year. SBA Advocacy reports 6.3 percent of small firms using AI against 11.1 percent of large firms six months earlier, rising only to 8.8 percent now. Source citations 01 plus 02.
The gap widened even as tools got cheaper. ICSB reports small firms doubling from 7 to 17.5 percent between 2023 and 2025 while large firms moved from 30.4 to 52.1 percent. Source citation 03.
Owners confirm the stall. NEXT surveyed 1,500 owners in April 2025: 28 percent use AI, down from 42 percent in 2024, with 58 percent planning no use at all. Barriers named are cost plus complexity. Source citation 04.
Local firms lag in marketing basics too. CMO Council finds local companies woefully behind national brands, with about half underinvesting in mainstream marketing and 50 percent plus lacking confidence across social management. Source citation 05.
Effect: a 2 minute bottleneck score feels novel to this audience, while digital natives have seen ten quizzes like it.

Reason 2. More value per hour freed.
Admin load is heaviest where no team absorbs it. Sage plus Plum Consulting finds business builders spend 120 working days per year on administration, with small firms at 230 to 240 days, or 17 percent of manpower. Source citation 06.
German panel data agrees. KfW finds SMEs spend 7 percent of working time, about 32 hours per month, on admin, with solo owners doing everything alone. Source citation 07.
Owners feel the shortage daily. An Independent poll of 500 SMEs finds the average owner needs 4 extra hours per day for admin, with 33 percent short on minutes. Source citation 08.
Payroll alone drains mornings. Intuit plus Kelton finds owners spend nearly 5 hours per pay period on payroll, with 65 percent preferring to outsource it. Source citation 09.
Effect: at 175 USD per founder hour, the 39,000 Year 1 plan breaks even at 4.3 hours per week, against 12.5 saved. Math lives in icp_personas_qualifier_math.md.

Reason 3. Willing to spend on outside help.
Budgets already flow outward. Clutch 2023 finds 83 percent of small firms will maintain or increase outsourcing spend, surveyed across 517 leaders. Source citation 10.
The trend rises yearly. Clutch 2022 finds 90 percent plan to outsource, up from 80 percent in 2021, across 1,003 owners. Top motives are saving time plus tapping experts. Source citation 11.
Compliance spend proves the habit. KfW finds external providers cover 34.6 percent of SME compliance hours, with only 19 percent using no provider at all. Source citation 07.
Owners buy back selling time. The US Chamber notes outsourcing frees owners for revenue work, with accounting plus marketing plus support among the most outsourced functions. Source citation 12.
Effect: the email CTA asks for a habit they already practice, with no new budget category to invent.

Reason 4. Less saturated than the digital entrepreneur market.
Generalist agencies overlook this segment. BidWaves states local firms are underserved by agencies too big to care. Source citation 13.
Enterprise focus leaves trades behind. Red Tri Digital notes larger agencies chase enterprise clients while florists plus dog trainers plus independent shops plus trades get overlooked. Source citation 14.
Serving everyone serves no one. Cordero Digital states most agencies mix e commerce plus startups plus corporates plus local firms into generic playbooks. Source citation 15.
National tactics do not scale local. CMO Council finds local partners left to fend for themselves, with digital tactics built for national markets failing at street level. Source citation 05.
Effect: inbox plus calendar plus SOPs plus follow ups language lands fresh here, while digital feeds drown in identical hooks.

Funnel consequences, locked:
Qualifier stays 300k plus plus exploring AI help. Copy speaks admin hours plus missed follow ups plus owner fatigue, with no online jargon. Proof leads with retention plus matches. Booking stays email CTA, matching how these owners already buy.
