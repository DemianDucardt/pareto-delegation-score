Title: Competitor Teardowns. Owner Demian Ducardt. Date 2026-10-06.

1. Ryan Deiss spreadsheet giveaway. Shown live by Ivan Bunin Day 10.
Format: free Google Sheet. Promise: run 17 companies from one sheet.
Who for: operators drowning in reports.
Capture: first name, email, company size, revenue band.
After opt in: instant access page plus step 3 book call with availability.
Gap: no score. No AI angle. Generic sheet.

2. BELAY delegation checklist.
Format: PDF checklist. Promise: hand off 50 tasks this week.
Who for: US founders with admin overload.
Capture: name, email, role, team size.
After opt in: PDF by email plus 3 nurture emails to sales call.
Gap: static list. No scoring. No workflow proof.

3. Dan Martell Buyback funnel.
Format: book plus quiz. Promise: buy back time with replacement ladder.
Who for: founders at 500k plus doing 100 USD plus per hour work.
Capture: name, email, revenue, bottleneck quiz.
After opt in: book sample plus call invite.
Gap: book first, action second. Slow payoff.

Takeaway for Pareto:
Score in 2 minutes beats PDF in 20 minutes.
Quiz routes companies behind on AI to email CTA fast.
